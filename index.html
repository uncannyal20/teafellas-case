Tea Fellas — Case Briefing
"Good Tea. Good Day."  ·  #01-08, Block 190 Toa Payoh Lorong 6, Singapore 310190  ·  Mon–Fri 7:30am–8pm  ·  Est. 2008
Background

Uncle John DeSouza (61) built Tea Fellas after being retrenched in his 40s from a long logistics career. Rather than return to corporate life, he travelled to Taiwan and Japan — inspired by the craft of tea culture there — and came home with a clear vision for a quality, community tea shop.

He opened in 2008 in Toa Payoh, where he had lived for decades. His wife Aunty Mary (59) quit her own corporate job to run the counter with him. Their two daughters are based in Australia. The shop survived COVID-19 in 2020 and retained its loyal customer base.

Tea Fellas is known for its quality, less-sweet, authentic flavours — and for its signature Brown Sugar Pearl Milk Tea, with tapioca pearls handmade fresh daily by Darren.

Where things stand: Tea Fellas has built a loyal following and is at a crossroads. John has many ideas for where to take the business — but he is unsure which direction to pursue first, or even how to begin. He has reached out for help thinking it through.
