"Before you pick a solution, make sure you understand the numbers. Uncle John's net monthly surplus is only $4,100 — that's what we have left after everything. Any option you recommend has to be one we can actually afford, not just one that sounds good on paper. My advice: look at each option carefully, compare the startup cost and the monthly commitment, and ask yourself — if we have one slow month, can we still pay our Toa Payoh rent? Start with what's real. Then build up from there."
