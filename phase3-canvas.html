3.3.1 — Solution Concept Canvas
Phase 3 — Ideate · Capture your solution concept across six dimensions
1
Customer Need
What is the core problem or unmet need?
What evidence do we have?
Who experiences this most acutely?
2
Value Proposition
What do customers get from this solution?
How does it directly answer their need?
Why would they choose this over alternatives?
3
Solution Overview
What are we proposing to build or change?
How does it meet the need identified?
What is explicitly out of scope?
4
Key Features
Must have (Day 1 essentials)
Nice to have (Phase 2+)
Release / go-live criteria
5
Expected Outcomes
What changes for the customer?
What changes for the business?
How does this connect to the long-term vision?
6
Success Measures
What will we measure?
What are our targets?
How and when will we check progress?
