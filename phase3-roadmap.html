Phase 3 — Ideate · 18-month Gantt chart delivery plan for your recommended scenario
How to complete this artefact
Download the PowerPoint template and build your 18-month Gantt chart directly in PowerPoint. Shade cells to show delivery windows for each workstream.
  1. Download the template and open in PowerPoint
  2. Add your workstream names in the left column (e.g. Vendor Selection, Development, UAT, Staff Training, Go-Live)
  3. Shade month cells to show when each workstream runs — use colour to show phase
  4. Mark key milestones (◆) in the relevant month cell
