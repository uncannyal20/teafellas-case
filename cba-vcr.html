Scenario narratives
Why each scenario is or isn't the right call for Tea Fellas.
A1
Basic digital — Solid MVP, limited upside
Strong BCR (3.1×) and low risk make A1 an attractive first step. Fixes 8 of 9 pain points and pays back in under 14 months. The gap: no expansion, no supply chain resolution, and no growth story. Recommended only if budget is extremely constrained.
A2
Full integrated — Maximum ops value, not standalone viable
A2 solves every operational pain point including supply chain — the only scenario that does. But at $150K against a $4,100 monthly surplus, it takes over 4 years to pay back and has a negative 3-year ROI. Only viable with significant grant or loan funding (>40% offset).
B1
AMK Food Centre — Revenue growth, operational risk
Expanding without fixing operations exports current chaos to a second location. Darren is clear: "Two chaotic shops instead of one." B1 is a viable expansion format (lower rent, familiar customer profile) but should never precede A1. On its own, not recommended.
B2
AMK Hub B1 — High potential, existential financial risk
$20,000 per month in additional costs against a $4,100 monthly surplus is an existential commitment. Even with strong Year 3 revenue projections, a slow ramp-up at the Hub puts the entire business at risk. Not recommended without a clear external funding source.
C1 ⭐ Recommended
Conservative — The right balance of value, cost, and risk
C1 fixes operations first (A1), proves the digital model over 6 months, then expands to AMK Food Centre at Month 12. BCR of 2.75×, payback in Year 2, and a Year 3 combined surplus of ~$16,250/month. The sequential approach converts B1's risk into a managed milestone. Accepted trade-off: supply chain (#9) deferred to Year 2 upgrade.
C2
Aggressive — Correct direction, wrong financials
C2 would be the ideal solution in a well-funded context — it resolves all 9 pain points and maximises long-term revenue. But $300,000 upfront on a $4,100 monthly surplus requires a $300K business loan, which is not realistic for a family-run heartland shop without external investors or grant support.
How to read VCR
Value — How much does this scenario improve operations, customer experience, and growth potential?
Cost — What is the total financial commitment relative to Tea Fellas' $4,100/month surplus?
Risk — How likely is implementation to fail, and how severe would failure be for the business?

The recommended scenario is the one that maximises Value while keeping Cost and Risk within what a small family business can absorb. That is C1.
