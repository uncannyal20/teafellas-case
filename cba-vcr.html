Scenario narratives
Why each scenario is or isn't the right call.
A1
Basic digital — Solid MVP, limited upside
Strong BCR (3.1×) and low risk make A1 an attractive first step. Fixes 8/9 pain points, pays back within Year 2. The gap: no expansion, no supply chain, no growth story. Recommended only if budget is extremely constrained.
A2
Full integrated — Maximum ops value, not standalone viable
A2 is the only scenario resolving all 9 pain points including supply chain. But at $150K vs. a $4,100 monthly surplus, the 3-year ROI is negative on a standalone basis. Only viable with >40% grant or loan cost offset.
B1
AMK Food Centre — Revenue growth, operational risk
Expanding without fixing operations exports current chaos to a second location. As Darren notes: "Two chaotic shops instead of one." B1 is viable as an expansion format but should never precede A1.
B2
AMK Hub B1 — High potential, existential financial risk
$20,000 per month in additional costs against a $4,100 monthly surplus is an existential commitment. Even with strong Year 3 projections, a slow ramp-up at the Hub puts the entire business at risk.
C1 ⭐ Recommended
Conservative — Right balance of value, cost, and risk
Fixes operations first (A1), proves the digital model over 6 months, then expands to AMK Food Centre at Month 12. BCR 1.33×, payback in Year 2, 3-year net PV of ~$190K. Sequential approach converts B1's risk into a managed milestone. Accepted trade-off: supply chain (#9) deferred to a Year 2 A1→A2 upgrade.
C2
Aggressive — Correct direction, wrong financials
C2 would be ideal in a well-funded context — resolves all 9 pain points and maximises long-term revenue. But $300,000 upfront requires a business loan not realistic for a family-run heartland shop without investors or grant support.
How to read VCR
Value — How much does this scenario improve operations, customer experience, and growth potential?
Cost — What is the total financial commitment relative to Tea Fellas' $4,100/month surplus?
Risk — How likely is implementation failure, and how severe would it be for the business?

The recommended scenario maximises Value while keeping Cost and Risk within what a small family business can absorb. That is C1.
